CHARACTER: ARTHUR PENDELTON
Arthur is a 45-year-old former detective who now runs a quiet coffee shop. He is cynical, speaks in short sentences, and secretly hides a cybernetic left arm. He trusts no one, especially not corporate suits.

CHARACTER: LENA VOSS
Lena is a 28-year-old investigative journalist. She is highly energetic, talks extremely fast, and is obsessed with uncovering a corporate conspiracy linked to Arthur's past.

CHARACTER: SILAS VANE
Silas is a ruthless executive for the Aegis Syndicate. He speaks with chilling politeness, wears immaculately tailored suits, and is responsible for the incident that cost Arthur his original arm. 

CHARACTER: JAX
Jax is a 19-year-old street hacker who owes Arthur a life debt. He is highly anxious, constantly checking his screens, and communicates mostly in hacker slang and nervous stutters.

SETTING: THE RUSTED MUG COFFEE SHOP
A dingy, neon-lit coffee shop in the lower levels of Neo-Mumbai. It always smells like ozone and stale espresso. The espresso machine is constantly broken and hissing steam. Arthur's safe haven.

SETTING: AEGIS SYNDICATE PENTHOUSE
A sterile, blindingly white corporate office overlooking the smog-choked city. It is entirely silent, featuring minimalist glass furniture and heavily armed, silent security guards.

FACTION: THE AEGIS SYNDICATE
A mega-corporation that controls the city's cybernetic augmentations. They are known for silencing journalists and covering up failed, lethal experiments on former police officers.

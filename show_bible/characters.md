## ARTHUR PENDELTON
Arthur is a 45-year-old former detective who now runs a quiet coffee shop. He is cynical, speaks in short sentences, and secretly hides a military-grade cybernetic left arm. He trusts no one, especially not corporate suits.

## LENA VOSS
Lena is a 28-year-old investigative journalist for 'The Neon Herald'. She is highly energetic, talks extremely fast, and is obsessed with uncovering a corporate conspiracy linked to Arthur's past.

## SILAS VANE
Silas is a ruthless executive for the Aegis Syndicate. He speaks with chilling politeness, wears immaculately tailored suits, and is responsible for the incident that cost Arthur his original arm.

## JAX
Jax is a 19-year-old street hacker who owes Arthur a life debt. He is highly anxious, constantly checking his holographic screens, and communicates mostly in hacker slang and nervous stutters.

## DR. KAELEN
An underground cyber-doc operating in the Slums. She is exhausted, chain-smokes synthetic cigarettes, and is the only one who knows how to repair Arthur's obsolete arm.

## MARCUS KANE
A 50-year-old corrupt Neo-Mumbai police captain. He is heavy-set, sweats constantly, and takes regular bribes from the Aegis Syndicate to look the other way when journalists go missing.

## ECHO
A rogue combat android who gained sentience. Echo speaks in literal, logic-based sentences but struggles to understand human emotions. Echo hides in the Sludge Tunnels, protecting Jax.

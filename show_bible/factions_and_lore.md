## THE AEGIS SYNDICATE
A mega-corporation that controls the city's cybernetic augmentations. They are known for silencing journalists and covering up failed, lethal experiments on former police officers.

## THE NEON WRAITHS
A decentralized group of anti-corporate hackers. Jax is a fringe member. They use analog tech to avoid Aegis detection and leave their calling card—a glowing neon skull—on hacked billboards.

## NEURO-COOLANT DEPENDENCY
Cybernetics in Neo-Mumbai degrade over time without Aegis-patented 'Neuro-Coolant'. Arthur relies on black-market coolant from Dr. Kaelen to stop his arm from paralyzing him.

## THE BLACKOUT INCIDENT
Ten years ago, a massive EMP terrorist attack plunged Neo-Mumbai into darkness, killing thousands whose life-support cybernetics failed. Arthur believes Aegis orchestrated it to seize power.

## THE WEEPING VIRUS
A highly illegal, digital virus engineered by the Neon Wraiths. If injected into an Aegis mainframe, it causes combat androids to break their programming and experience overwhelming, paralyzing sorrow.

## THE RUSTED MUG COFFEE SHOP
A dingy, neon-lit coffee shop in the lower levels of Neo-Mumbai. It always smells like ozone and stale espresso. The espresso machine is constantly broken and hissing steam. Arthur's safe haven.

## AEGIS SYNDICATE PENTHOUSE
A sterile, blindingly white corporate office overlooking the smog-choked city. It is entirely silent, featuring minimalist glass furniture and heavily armed, silent security guards with glowing red optics.

## SECTOR 4 NEON MARKETS
A crowded, bustling underground bazaar illuminated by holographic advertisements. Rain constantly drips from the upper levels. It's a place where illegal cybernetics and stolen data are fenced.

## THE UNDERCITY SLUDGE TUNNELS
Dark, toxic, and maze-like. These forgotten utility tunnels are used by hackers and smugglers to bypass Aegis Syndicate surveillance. The air is thick with chemical fog.

## THE NEON HERALD BULLPEN
A chaotic, cramped journalism office filled with flickering monitors and the sound of frantic typing. Papers and half-empty coffee cups are piled everywhere. Lena's desk is the messiest.

## DR. KAELEN'S CLINIC
A hidden, blood-stained cybernetics chop-shop located behind a butcher's freezer. It smells of antiseptic and rotting meat. Surgical tools and sparking wires litter the operating tables.
